# 107.5 FM: music provenance and licences

Internal record for every audio file served by the radio
(`public/audio/radio/`). Public credits, as the licences require, are on
the site at **بيانات البث** (`/broadcast.html`, linked from every page's
colophon).

All files were checked at their source before download and are served
**unmodified** (byte-identical to the source file; SHA-1 compared), except
`radio-04.mp3`, which is Wikimedia Commons' own MP3 rendering of an Ogg
original (see below).

Accessed and downloaded: **7 October 2026**.

## Why these licences allow this website

All seven are under Creative Commons **Attribution-ShareAlike** (BY-SA)
licences. They allow anyone to copy and redistribute the work in any medium
or format, for any purpose, including hosting the file on our server and
streaming it to visitors, provided that:

1. **Attribution**: credit the author, the title, the source and the
   licence (with a link). → done on `/broadcast.html`.
2. **ShareAlike**: applies only to adaptations (remixes, edits). We do not
   adapt the recordings; we play them unchanged, so our own site content is
   not affected. A format change is allowed: CC BY-SA 2.5 §3 and 3.0 §3
   permit "modifications as are technically necessary to exercise the
   rights in other media and formats".
3. No technological restrictions: we add none (plain MP3 files over HTTPS).

Licences are irrevocable for copies obtained while the licence applied.

## Considered and rejected

| Candidate | Why not |
|---|---|
| Umm Kulthum, «ألف ليلة وليلة» (local MP3) | Commercial recording; no evidence of a licence to host. Not used, not copied. |
| 1928–1931 Arab/Turkish 78 rpm recordings on Commons (e.g. "Taxim Hicaz", "Art-song Maqam Sika", "Baschrav Kuzum Maqam Hijaz") | Tagged `{{PD-traditional}}`, which covers the traditional composition, not the recording. Sound recordings published 1923–1946 remain protected in the US for 100 years from publication (Music Modernization Act), so the recordings themselves are not cleared. |
| Pre-1923 vocal 78s (Munira al-Mahdiyya c. 1914; Mohamed Effendi El-Achek c. 1908/1909; Sheikh Sayed El-Safti 1907–1925) | Publication dates are approximate or ranges (the 1907–1925 range crosses 1923); also vocal, which we avoid for reading. Not used. |
| Mohamed El Fares (Qatar Digital Library) | `{{PD-old-70}}` concerns the author's death, not the recording ("before 1948"). Not cleared. |
| Internet Archive "Turkish Instrumental Music" (cl0udn0te, labelled CC0) | Re-upload of Pixabay files by an anonymous account with a different licence attached. Provenance unreliable. |
| Anonymous Internet Archive uploads ("Oriental Nights", "Taksim & Mawachah Chamoulo") | No identifiable creator; licence cannot be traced to the rights holder. |
